# 03:17

> **A 3D Psychological Horror Game**

**03:17** is a first-person 3D psychological horror game built with **Unity**.
The game focuses on exploration, atmosphere, environmental storytelling, survival, and psychological tension.

The title **03:17** represents a mysterious time connected to the events occurring throughout the game.

---

## 🎮 Game Overview

The player finds themselves trapped in a mysterious environment where strange events begin to occur at **03:17 AM**.

As the player explores the surroundings, they must:

* Explore the environment and discover hidden clues.
* Interact with objects around the map.
* Collect and manage important items.
* Solve puzzles to progress.
* Discover the story through environmental details.
* Survive unexpected events and encounters.
* Uncover the mystery behind **03:17**.

The game emphasizes **atmosphere and psychological horror** rather than relying entirely on jump scares.

---

## 🎯 Core Gameplay

### Exploration

Players explore a dark and mysterious environment while searching for items, clues, and hidden areas.

### Interaction

Objects can be examined and interacted with to reveal information or affect the environment.

### Inventory

Collected items are stored in the player's inventory and can be used to solve puzzles or progress through the game.

### Puzzle Solving

Players must combine information and collected items to solve environmental puzzles.

### Horror & Atmosphere

The game uses:

* Dark environments
* Lighting
* Ambient sounds
* Environmental storytelling
* Unexpected events
* Psychological tension

to create a frightening atmosphere.

---

## 📖 Story

At exactly **03:17 AM**, something unusual begins to happen.

The player gradually realizes that the environment is not what it initially appears to be. Strange sounds, unexplained events, and hidden clues lead deeper into the mystery.

The player must investigate the surroundings and uncover what happened at **03:17**.

> **What really happened at 03:17?**
